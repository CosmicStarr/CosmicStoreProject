Notice of Shipping Delays (FTC 30-Day Rule Compliance)

If we are unable to ship your order within the estimated timeframe provided at checkout, or within 30 days of receiving your completed order if no timeframe was provided, we will notify you by email. This notification will include:

  • The reason for the delay.
  • A revised estimated shipping date.
  • Your option to either consent to the delay or cancel your order for a prompt and full refund.

Customer Consent and Automatic Cancellations

If the revised shipping delay is 30 days or less, your silence will be treated as consent to the delay. If the revised shipping delay is more than 30 days, or if we are unable to provide a revised shipping date, we must obtain your explicit consent to the delay. If you do not explicitly consent to the extended delay, your order will be automatically canceled and fully refunded.

Fulfillment and Third-Party Logistics

To provide competitive pricing, your order will be fulfilled and shipped directly from our overseas suppliers. As the buyer, you are the importer of record and are responsible for any applicable customs duties, import taxes, or local clearance fees that may arise upon entry into the United States.

The price at checkout does not include those import charges, or similar fees that a carrier or government may assess for other destinations. Refusing a package because of unexpected import fees does not automatically entitle you to a refund of the merchandise or of return shipping.
