Refunds follow our supplier

CosmicStore is fulfilled by overseas partners, including CJ Dropshipping. We refund through Stripe when the supplier covers the claim. We do not refund out of pocket for reasons CJ will not accept. You may still cancel and receive a full refund if the supplier has not shipped yet.

All sales are final after shipment unless an item arrives damaged or defective, is the wrong item or is missing parts, or is lost or delayed under the timelines below. Change of mind, “I ordered the wrong thing,” unusual smell, or not liking the product are not refundable after shipment.

Damaged or defective items

Badly damaged items may receive a full refund or replacement. Slight damage may receive a partial refund, matching the supplier’s decision. Open this claim within {{ qualityDays }} days of delivery.

Submit a refund request that describes the damage. We will email you where to send a clear photo or video of the damage (and a screenshot of your complaint if you wrote to us separately). We file that evidence with CJ. A Stripe refund is issued after the supplier accepts the dispute.

Lost or delayed orders

If a shipment has no tracking updates or is excessively delayed, the supplier may refund or resend it. For U.S. orders, CJ treats a package as delayed only after {{ lostDays }} days from the time it left their warehouse. We cannot issue a lost-package refund before that date.

If tracking shows “delivered” but you did not receive the package, CJ requires an official non-delivery certificate from your local post office. We will ask you for that document before we can process the claim.

Wrong items or missing parts

If the supplier ships the wrong color, size, or a missing part, they may refund or resend it. File within {{ qualityDays }} days of delivery. Describe what is wrong and send a photo of the item you received (include measurements if the issue is size). We submit that evidence to CJ and refund through Stripe if they accept the claim.

Buyer’s remorse

After the supplier ships, we do not refund because you changed your mind, ordered the wrong item, or dislike the product. Those reasons are not covered by CJ. Cancel before shipment if you no longer want the order.

Do not ship returns to China

Do not mail merchandise back to an overseas warehouse. International returns are expensive, slow, and often lost, and they create a poor experience. Do not ship a return to a CosmicStore personal address unless we email you a domestic address for that specific order. If we ask for a domestic return, we will tell you where to send it and what tracking to submit.
