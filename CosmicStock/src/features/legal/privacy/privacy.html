Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026 · Version {{ LEGAL_TERMS_VERSION }}

This policy explains what CosmicStore collects when you browse, check out, create an account, or share a gift registry, and how that information moves through our custom API, database, payment processor, and fulfillment partners.

Information we collect

  • Account. If you register, we store your email, display name, hashed password, saved delivery addresses, wishlist, order history, and the Terms version and timestamp you accepted.
  • Guest checkout. We do not create a stored customer account. We still need the email and shipping details you enter so we can place the order, send confirmation mail, and let you manage the order from the private link we send.
  • Orders. Recipient name, email, delivery address, product lines, amounts, shipping method, payment status, supplier shipment identifiers, and your Terms acceptance timestamp for that order.
  • Cart. A cart identifier is stored in your browser so the cart can be restored. Cart contents are held in Redis on our servers, not in the browser.
  • Session and security. If you sign in, a session record is stored in your browser so we can keep you signed in. Guest checkout sessions are not persisted that way. We may use IP address with Redis to rate-limit guest order lookups and reduce abuse.

Payment processing

CosmicStore does not store raw credit card numbers, CVV codes, or full card PAN data on our servers. Card details are entered in Stripe’s payment form and sent directly to Stripe. Stripe may use a billing ZIP or postal code to verify the card. That billing ZIP is not used as a gift-registry delivery address.

Data storage and security

Account, address, wishlist, and order records are stored in our SQL Server database. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes by ASP.NET Identity, not as plain text. Traffic to the storefront and API uses HTTPS. Redis holds short-lived operational data: shopping carts, catalog-sync markers, and security throttles (including guest-order verification limits keyed by IP address).

Third-party fulfillment sharing

CosmicStore is a dropshipping storefront. To fulfill an order we share shipping information with third-party logistics partners and overseas suppliers, including CJ Dropshipping. That typically includes the recipient’s name, delivery address, contact email, and the products purchased. We do not currently collect a phone number at checkout. If a supplier later requires a contact number and you have provided one on your account, that number may also be shared so the shipment can be delivered.

Gift registry privacy

If you share a wishlist as a gift registry, the public page and the buyer’s checkout show only a masked label (for example, “Ship to Alex’s Registry Address”). Street, city, and ZIP are hidden from the buyer. The real registry address is still stored in our SQL Server database, applied on our servers after payment, and shared with CosmicStore staff and with fulfillment partners (including CJ Dropshipping) so the gift can be delivered.

Email

We send transactional mail only: order confirmation, guest order-management links, password reset, email confirmation, email-change alerts, and cancellation or refund notices. We do not sell your email address.

How long we keep it

Order, fulfillment, and Terms-acceptance records are kept as long as we need them to complete shipping, cancellations, refunds, accounting, and payment disputes. Guest access links are invalidated after a successful Stripe refund for that guest order. You can update or delete saved addresses from your profile while signed in, unless an address is still tied to an active gift registry.

Your choices

  • Shop as a guest without creating an account.
  • Change or confirm your account email from your profile.
  • Sign out to end the stored browser session.
  • Use the private guest-order link from your confirmation email to view or request cancellation or a refund when the order rules allow it.

Contact

For a privacy question about an order, reply using the email address on your confirmation message, or use guest order management / signed-in order history. Related store rules are in the Terms & Conditions.
