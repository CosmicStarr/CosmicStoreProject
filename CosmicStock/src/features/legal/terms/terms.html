Gift registries

A public gift registry hides the recipient’s street, city, and ZIP from buyers. Checkout for those items locks shipping to the registry destination. The address is still stored in our database and is still shared with CosmicStore and with logistics partners, including CJ Dropshipping, so the gift can be delivered. Do not share a registry if you are not willing to have that address used for fulfillment.

Cancellations before shipment

You may request cancellation before the supplier ships. If the supplier can cancel the unshipped order, we refund the Stripe payment. Refunds typically appear on the original payment method in 3–5 business days.
