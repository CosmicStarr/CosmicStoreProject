About CosmicStore

An online catalog for everyday products, shipped through our fulfillment partners.

CosmicStore is a storefront where you can browse the catalog, save a wishlist, and check out as a guest or with an account. Orders are paid with Stripe and fulfilled by third-party logistics partners, including CJ Dropshipping.

Shopping

  • Browse featured picks on the home page or search the full catalog.
  • Keep items in your cart while you shop. Guest checkout does not require creating an account.
  • Signed-in shoppers can save addresses, view order history, and keep a wishlist.

Gift registries

Turn a wishlist into a gift registry so friends and family can buy from your list. Buyers see a masked destination such as “Ship to Alex’s Registry Address,” not the street, city, or ZIP. The delivery address is still used by CosmicStore and our fulfillment partners so the gift can arrive.

Orders and help

After you pay, we email a confirmation to the address you entered at checkout. Signed-in customers can track orders from their account. Guest shoppers can manage an order with the private link from that email.
